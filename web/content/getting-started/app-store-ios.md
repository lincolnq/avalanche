Avalanche Messenger is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

<p><a class="btn btn-primary" href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/avalanche-messenger/id6772672507" rel="noopener">Get it on the App Store</a></p>

## Install it

1. Tap the **Get it on the App Store** link above on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Tap **Get**, then open Avalanche from your home screen.
